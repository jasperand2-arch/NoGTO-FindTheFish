# NoGTO FindTheFish

Most small-stakes players think the game starts when the cards are dealt.

It doesn't.

**FindTheFish** is an evidence-first study of where small-stakes poker EV actually comes from.

I analysed millions of real PokerStars hands across 10NL, 25NL and 50NL, then looked at 30 of the largest established winners to answer a simple question:

> Where are winning players actually making their money?

The answer changed the direction of the project.

Roughly 70% of the measured profit in the Top-30 sample came from opponents playing 40%+ VPIP.

The clean heads-up confrontation rates across those winners were approximately:

| Opponent VPIP | bb/100 |
|---|---:|
| <30 | +17.6 |
| 30–39 | +100.4 |
| 40–59 | +149.8 |
| 60%+ | +205.1 |

These are clean heads-up-flop confrontation rates, not ordinary all-hands winrates.

The important point is the shape of the data.

The loosest opponents were not slightly more valuable.

They were multiples more valuable.

## What this is about

FindTheFish looks at the part of poker strategy that usually gets reduced to a sentence:

> "Try to play with weaker players."

I wanted to go further.

Who should you target?

How valuable are they?

How frequently do they appear?

How do the biggest winners gain access to them?

How should you play against 40–54 VPIP passive players?

How does that change against 55%+ VPIP very passive players?

When should you isolate?

When should you stop bluffing?

Which river changes actually generate folds?

And what happens when another regular is aggressively trying to isolate the same weak player?

The final section also looks at how to counter the "whale hunters" themselves.

## Conversational MDA

This project was built using **Conversational MDA**, developed on top of my own mass-data-analysis platform.

Instead of beginning with a solver and asking what players should do, the process begins with what players actually did.

The governing idea is:

1. Empirical evidence is the baseline knowledge.
2. Measured behaviour, evidence quality and economics establish the exploit.
3. Narrower evidence replaces broader priors only when it earns greater authority.
4. GTO is the optional fallback when empirical evidence cannot resolve the decision.

No solver was required to prove that the mistakes existed.

The evidence did that.

## Why this is free

I'm giving FindTheFish away because I want small-stakes players to see the game that is actually being played around them.

Poker isn't a sport where everyone is required to take the same opponents and prove who plays the most theoretically correct strategy.

It is an EV game.

Table selection, seat selection, opponent selection, isolation and knowing when to leave are not things that happen around poker.

They are part of poker.

A large amount of EV can be created before the cards are dealt.

I want more players fighting over those edges again.
